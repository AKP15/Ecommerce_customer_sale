# E-Commerce Customer & Sales Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce transaction data to understand revenue performance, customer behavior, product categories, sales channels, and product returns.

The goal is to identify important business trends and provide actionable recommendations for management.

## Dataset

- 1,000 transactions
- 13 columns
- Transaction and customer-related information
- Sales, returns, ratings, category, channel, country, and payment data

## Key KPIs

- Total Revenue: $290,375.26
- Total Orders: 1,000
- Quantity Sold: 3,049
- Average Order Value: $290.38
- Return Rate: 6.40%
- Average Rating: 3.83/5

## Key Findings

### Revenue by Category

Electronics generated $148,969.01, representing approximately 51.3% of total revenue.

Beauty was the weakest category with $15,626.30.

### Monthly Revenue

August was the strongest month with $29,400.15.

April was the weakest month with $20,782.45.

Revenue fluctuated throughout the year without a consistent upward trend.

### Returns

Sports had the highest category return rate at 8.55%.

Electronics through the Mobile App had the highest Category + Channel return rate at 9.71%.

### Customer Ratings

Returned orders had an average rating of 3.69 compared with 3.84 for non-returned orders.

The correlation between Sales and Rating was -0.041, indicating almost no linear relationship.

## Dashboard

The dashboard includes:

## Recommendations

1. Reduce dependence on Electronics revenue.
2. Investigate the high return rate in Sports.
3. Investigate Electronics returns through the Mobile App.
4. Investigate the causes of low April revenue.
5. Continue monitoring customer satisfaction and return behavior.
