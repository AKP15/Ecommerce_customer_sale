Executive Summary
The analysis of 1,000 e-commerce transactions generated $290,375.26 in revenue with an overall 6.40% return rate and an average customer rating of 3.83/5.
Key Findings
1. Electronics dominates revenue
Revenue: $148,969.01
Share: 51.3%
Electronics is the company's strongest category.
Beauty generated the lowest revenue at $15,626.30.
2. Revenue fluctuates throughout the year
Best month: August — $29,400.15
Worst month: April — $20,782.45
There is no consistent upward monthly trend.
3. Sports has the highest return rate
Sports: 8.55%
Clothing: 4.72%
Sports should be investigated for possible product-quality, expectation, sizing, or fulfillment issues.
4. Electronics + Mobile App is a high-risk combination
Return rate: 9.71%
This is the highest Category + Channel return rate.
Management should investigate the Mobile App electronics purchasing experience.
5. Sales value and customer rating are barely related
Correlation: −0.041
There is essentially no linear relationship between order value and rating.


Recommendations
Reduce dependence on Electronics by developing other categories.
Investigate Sports returns.
Investigate Electronics purchases through the Mobile App.
Analyze why April revenue is significantly lower.
Continue monitoring customer ratings and returns.
